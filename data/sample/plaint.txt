SUIT FOR SPECIFIC PERFORMANCE AND PERMANENT INJUNCTION

MOST RESPECTFULLY SHOWETH:

1. That the plaintiff and the defendant entered into an agreement to sell in respect
   of commercial property bearing Plot No. 47, Block C, Okhla Industrial Estate,
   New Delhi, admeasuring 4,500 sq. ft. The said agreement to sell was executed on
   12.03.2016 for a total sale consideration of Rs. 3,80,00,000/- (Rupees Three
   Crore Eighty Lakhs only).

2. That in part performance of the said agreement, the plaintiff paid a sum of
   Rs. 95,00,000/- as earnest money on the date of execution, receipt whereof was
   duly acknowledged by the defendant.

3. That the plaintiff has at all material times been ready and willing to perform
   his part of the contract and continues to be so ready and willing. The plaintiff
   repeatedly called upon the defendant to execute the sale deed and to complete the
   transaction.

4. That the defendant committed breach of the said agreement on 04.06.2019 by
   addressing a communication refusing to execute the sale deed and purporting to
   forfeit the earnest money paid by the plaintiff.

5. That the correspondence between the parties, including WhatsApp messages
   exchanged between the proprietor of the plaintiff and the managing director of
   the defendant during the period 2016 to 2019, evidences the defendant's
   acknowledgment of the agreement and its subsequent refusal. The plaintiff relies
   upon printouts of the said electronic records.

6. That the cause of action arose on 04.06.2019 when the defendant refused to
   perform, and continues to subsist.

7. That the suit was filed on 18.01.2021 before this Hon'ble Court, the subject
   matter being a commercial dispute of a specified value within the meaning of the
   Commercial Courts Act 2015.

PRAYER

It is therefore most respectfully prayed that this Hon'ble Court may be pleased to:

(a) pass a decree of specific performance directing the defendant to execute the
    sale deed in respect of the suit property in favour of the plaintiff;

(b) grant a permanent injunction restraining the defendant from creating any third
    party interest in the suit property;

(c) award costs of the suit.
